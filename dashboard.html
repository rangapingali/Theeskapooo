Before you hit print.

Keep your layout intact

PDF is best for assignments and reports. Word, slides and spreadsheets are accepted with a manual page count, but their layout can change on another computer. PDF is recommended when layout must stay exact.

Know your page count

Each still image counts as one page; multipage TIFF files include every detected page. PDF and supported image pages are counted automatically. Office documents and other formats ask for a page count from Print Preview. The shop checks these counts before printing. Page ranges and copies update your price.

Pay your way

Pay at the counter, or pay online after placing your order. Offline orders keep the online option until collection. A pending payment is not a successful payment.

Collect when it’s ready

Wait for “Ready to collect” in My orders. Bring your order reference. Time estimates do not include the shop’s live queue.
